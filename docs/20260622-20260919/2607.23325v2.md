## 摘要
年龄是一种匹配特征，也是搜索平台上突出的标签。利用一个大型日本婚恋平台的机密记录，我研究了生日年龄更新如何影响考虑、申请、关系进展和订婚。只有显示的年龄会在生日时更新。接收者在退出某些可接受年龄范围的同时进入另一些范围，因此总体资格变化不大。申请数量通常会增加。申请从较年轻的追求者转向较年长的追求者。在十个女性接收者年龄中的九个（31–40岁），求婚数量下降，损失集中在进入早期约会阶段。在对三十多岁接收者进行分阶段核算时，无生日时的求婚数量比女性接收者的基准高12.9%，比男性接收者的基准低6.7%；女性接收者渠道中年轻男性占比高出4.8个百分点。年龄标签和筛选并非偏好的中性窗口：它们决定了谁与谁结婚以及形成多少婚姻。

## Abstract
Age is a match trait and a prominent label on search platforms. Using confidential records from a large Japanese marriage platform, I study how a birthday age update affects consideration, applications, relationship progression, and engagement. Only displayed age updates at birthdays. Receivers enter some acceptable-age ranges as they exit others, leaving only modest overall eligibility changes. Application counts often increase. Applications shift from younger to older suitors. Proposal counts fall at nine of ten female receiver ages \(31\text{--}40\), with losses concentrated at entry into early dating. In stage-specific accounting for receivers in their thirties, the no-birthday proposal count is \(12.9\) percent above the benchmark for female receivers and \(6.7\) percent below it for male receivers; the younger-man share in the female-receiver channel is \(4.8\) percentage points higher. Age labels and filters are not neutral windows onto preferences: they govern who marries whom and how many marriages form.
